Performance: Latency and WER depend on the speed–quality setting and source audio. The selector balances response time against recognition quality; lower WER is better.

Operation: Designed for continuous 24/7 use. Live captioning runs offline, independently of internet disruptions and cloud outages.

Installation: Validate performance, audio routing, hardware and recovery with studio material. Other production hosts, SDI, genlock and embedded closed captions require a separate assessment.
