The technology, simply.

Live studio audio is converted into Hebrew text by local AI speech recognition. The system arranges the text as readable right-to-left captions and delivers a visual overlay to your production workflow.

Installation: Audio routing, hardware environment, recovery procedures and performance are validated with representative studio material.

Integration: Video remains in your production chain. SDI, genlock and embedded closed captions require a separate assessment.
