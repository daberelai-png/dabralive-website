Accessibility statement

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Access to this website

We aim to make the daberelive website usable by as many people as possible. The site is available in Hebrew and English. There is no physical reception location; enquiries are handled remotely.

Implemented adaptations

The website includes keyboard navigation, a skip-to-content link, visible focus indicators, structured headings, image descriptions and responsive layouts. Decorative continuous animations have been disabled. Wide technical tables can be scrolled horizontally.

Checks and limitations

Checks covered keyboard use, selected text contrast and layouts at screen widths from 320 to 1440 pixels. These checks do not constitute a complete accessibility certification. Testing with screen readers, actual mobile devices and enlarged text remains to be completed. Some visual or interactive elements may still need improvement.

Accessibility enquiries

If you encounter a barrier or need information in an alternative format, email our support team. Please include the page address, a description of the issue and, if possible, the browser or assistive technology used. Avoid sending sensitive personal information.

support@wave22labs.com

Privacy information

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Scope and contact

This notice concerns the public daberelive website, presented under the Wave22 Labs brand. It does not describe data handling by a customer's deployed STT system. For website privacy enquiries, use the contact address below.

Information you send

Contact buttons open your email application. Sending an enquiry is voluntary. Your message may include your email address, name and any details you choose to provide, and is used to respond to and handle your request. Without contact details, we may be unable to reply. Please do not send confidential recordings or unnecessary sensitive information.

Hosting, security and cookies

The website uses Sites hosting and Cloudflare infrastructure. Infrastructure providers may process technical data such as IP addresses, request details and security logs, and may use essential security cookies. Email is handled through Google Workspace. Processing by these providers may take place outside Israel. No advertising or analytics code is intentionally included in the current website source.

Enquiries and your rights

Messages are directed to the team handling enquiries and processed through the email service. You can contact us to request access to or correction of personal information, or ask about deletion and retention. Requests will be considered according to applicable law and the circumstances of the enquiry. Contacting us does not by itself constitute consent to marketing messages.
