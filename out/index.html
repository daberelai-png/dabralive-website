LOCAL AI · OFFLINE BY DESIGN

Speech to captions.
Inside your studio.

The system converts a live Hebrew audio feed into readable captions using local AI speech recognition. Captions are presented in a right-to-left layout and delivered as a visual layer to the production workflow.

A dedicated physical appliance keeps recognition and caption generation inside the studio. The live workflow operates offline, without relying on internet connectivity or cloud-service availability.
